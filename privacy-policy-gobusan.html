Privacy Policy - Visit Busan

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Introduction

Visit Busan respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the application handles user data and what information may be accessed, stored or processed when using the app.

Data collected by the application

Visit Busan does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, payment information or authentication data.

The application does not sell, share or transfer personal user data to third parties.

Location data

Visit Busan may access device location information only to provide map-related features and location-based functionality when enabled by the user.

Location access is used only to improve the map experience, such as helping users view their position on the map or use navigation-related features.

The application does not store your location history and does not transmit your location to external servers.

Local storage

The application stores some preferences locally on your device using local storage technologies.

The stored information may include:

  • Your preferred application language.
  • Your saved favorite places inside the application.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to external services.

External services and websites

Visit Busan may provide links to external services such as map providers or websites related to tourist locations.

When you open these external services, their own privacy policies and data practices apply. Visit Busan does not control the information collected by these third-party services.

Third-party libraries

The application uses technical libraries required for features such as displaying maps, loading local data and opening external links.

These libraries are used only to provide application functionality. Visit Busan does not use third-party SDKs for advertising tracking, user profiling or selling personal information.

Data security

Visit Busan takes reasonable measures to protect user information. Local application data remains stored on the user's device.

Any communication with external websites or services initiated by the user is handled through secure connections when available.

User control

You can control permissions granted to Visit Busan through your Android device settings.

You may disable location permissions at any time. Some map features may no longer work correctly if required permissions are removed.

Children's privacy

Visit Busan does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not create user profiles.

Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features change or when legal requirements evolve.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the official application page.
